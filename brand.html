Using the logo

  • Use the full-color logo on white or light backgrounds, and the reverse logo on navy or other dark backgrounds.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as the letter O on every side.
  • Don't stretch, recolor, rotate or add effects to the logo. Pick the version that fits the background instead.
  • Keep the logo at least 1 inch wide in print and 120 pixels wide on screen, so "SOLAR ENERGY" stays readable. Below that, use the icon.
