Go Solar

Why solar still pays in California, and the ways to pay for it.

Benefits

Why go solar

Hedge against rising rates

Utility energy is expected to keep getting more expensive and is exposed to market swings. Solar locks in your cost.

Higher property value

Solar adds value to your home, and in California it's excluded from property tax reassessment.

Untaxed returns

Solar can outperform many long-term investments, and unlike dividends, your savings aren't taxed.

Power or hot water

Output comes as kilowatts that offset your electricity or therms that heat your water.

Local jobs

Solar supports installers, engineers, designers, distributors and manufacturers in our community.

A cleaner environment

Solar conserves natural resources and displaces emissions from fossil fuels.

Rebates & incentives

The federal credit has ended. Solar still pays.

The 30% federal Residential Clean Energy Credit for residential systems expired on December 31, 2025. Without it, payback takes a few years longer, but total lifetime savings remain substantial for most homes with good solar potential.

Most homeowners still come out ahead: net-positive savings, higher home value, and more control over their energy.

Deadline: January 1, 2027. Solar finished before then stays excluded from property tax reassessment. See every current rule in California Solar Rules.
Financing

Ways to pay

The sooner you install, the sooner your electric bill drops.

Cash

Savings you get sooner are worth more than the same savings later. Customers average a pre-tax internal rate of return of 25–35% over 25 years.

Credit Union Loan

$0 down, low fixed payments, 7, 12, 15 or 20-year terms. No escalators, no prepayment penalty, and free energy once it's paid off.

Home Equity (HELOC)

You may qualify for the mortgage interest deduction, and you pay down the balance at your own pace.

Credit Card

Many customers pay by card to earn travel or rewards points. Card processing fees apply.

Monitoring

Watch your savings in real time

See your system's generation anytime, anywhere, from your computer or phone, and share your energy production. The monitoring interface depends on your system type.
