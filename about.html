Who we are

Renewable energy, done right since the beginning

Vasco Solar is a full-service solar company serving homeowners and businesses throughout Southern California, primarily Orange County, with a focus on saving energy and reducing utility costs.

We design every type of solar application: solar electricity, solar pool heating, solar hot water and battery storage, each optimally designed for your unique situation. Innovative technology, top-of-the-line equipment, skilled technicians and our commitment to customers have kept us going strong, and word of mouth remains our main source of new business.
