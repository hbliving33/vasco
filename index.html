What we do

Every kind of solar, under one roof

Solar electricity, solar pool heating, solar hot water and battery storage, each one designed around your property, your usage and your goals.

Solar Electricity

Grid-tied PV systems that cut your electric bill, protect you from rate increases and raise property value without raising property taxes.

Solar Pool Heating

Extend your swim season and drastically reduce or eliminate pool-heating costs with proven Solar Industries collectors.

Solar Hot Water

Custom-designed domestic hot water systems that supply most of your home's hot water, starting around $5,500 to $7,000.

Battery Solutions

Certified FranklinWH, SolarEdge and Enphase installer: backup power for outages and stored solar for evenings.

Solar Re-Roofing

Licensed removal and reinstallation of your panels when you re-roof, with a two-year workmanship warranty.

Repair & Service

Inverters, batteries, panels, pumps, controls and leaks: we service most types of solar systems.
